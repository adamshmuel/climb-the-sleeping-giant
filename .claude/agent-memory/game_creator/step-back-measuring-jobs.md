Before calling a Play-job miss a world gap, check the job steers like a player (spec route, sideways correction) and look for an earlier passing Play of the same object (progress rows).

**Why:** Task 6a3 (2026-11-04) used three rounds and a step back on misses that came from fixed-heading steering. The same cap ladder had already passed on computer and iPhone (stuck-safety rows 59, 63). Ladder width (plan 7) had been stopped by Adam.

**How to apply:** in a step back, the smallest piece is often a better measuring try plus one real phone try, with no world change. Never re-open plan 7-style ladder widening without a real player fall.
