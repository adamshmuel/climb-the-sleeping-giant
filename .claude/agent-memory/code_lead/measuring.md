# Measuring

- Measure gaps to a mesh with raycasts: `GetPartBoundsInBox` uses its whole bounding box.
- A look-only part (CanQuery off) can't be hit by rays: measure it from `CFrame` and `Size`. When a task makes a part look-only, check every test and script that rays it first.
- Compare two sides of a slope at the same x (or the same distance along), and leave the new part out of a measure of the ground under it.
- Before ruling a check onto a new kind of piece, measure what its ray really hits on the built piece (one read-only call). Where the piece has a stricter test of its own, reuse it and skip the old one, with a comment.
- A reach check for an invisible walk part also prints its gap above the visible surface; a gap over 0.5 goes to designer before the build (Task 6a hover, 2026-11-04). Check each look word in a spec against its mockup at plan time.
- Sink depth: never along the face normal (measures through the whole limb) nor straight down only (misses sideways burial). Inside = 0 of 26 outward rays hit and 20+ inward hit; depth = shortest inward hit; test inside PER character part (whole-character outward rays hit his other parts and miss sinks); no ray-down covered check, it gives false hits under overhangs (2026-11-04, 6d).
- A "board per straight run" rule needs the run measured for bends first: 6a2 run 4-10 hid a 57-degree corner (P9/P10), so cut by search (fewest boards within the limit), and lower an overlapping board 0.05 against flicker (2026-11-04).
- A climb that lets go at the same height in every try: compare the end x with the climbable part's x range first. The cap ladder climbs only on its 2-stud truss; its Look rungs are 5.2 wide (6a3, 2026-11-04).
- SunkParts 3x3 face points miss sinks on long thin parts: the 34-stud boot rails showed 0 rows but dense points (every 0.85 along) sank 0.70/1.10, rungs 1.00 not 0.74. Price a sink fix from a dense read-only measure, and give long parts a dense test (5b, 2026-11-04).
- Before ruling a trim or move on a walk surface, grep `src/tests` and the spec for the part name: 5b1 cut a plank to 8.6 (Rule 36 says 9) and the landing off its pinned 11.2 x 7 (2026-11-04).
- Before closing a gap on one side, measure the mirror side and the whole part: 6b's "left gap under the plank end" was the same on the right, and a body fit under both planks along their whole length (PathCheck.roomToStand, 2026-11-04).
- A walk-map flood over the belly ramps needs `boxIgnores` = both `BellyRamp_*` folders, or the body box breaks the left ramp at Piece9-10 and a control route finds no belly. Always run a control flood (nothing banned) before trusting a "0 routes" (6c skin count, 2026-11-08).
- A "no route" walk-map count uses the 78° Play walk-off slope, not 70; run the control per side with the same link kinds as the check (6c A2: right boot top walk+climb reached nothing, only slides joined it to the plank, 2026-11-08).
- A height-gap check (seam or ridge rise above jump surfaces) also measures the walk parts its own task adds (ledges, strap ends) and ray-scans for solid tops reached only by jumping (Sock, Teacup); a y filter skipped both (p5 Task 13 ridge, product, 2026-11-08).
- A walk piece copied from mockup art passes the over-art test but can still miss the body: measure where the art itself meets the cloth and the ledge before ruling the test wrong (p5t13 strap, 2.2 studs outboard).
- A walk piece copied from mockup art can pass the over-art test and still miss the body: measure where the art itself meets the cloth and the ledge before calling a test or the world wrong (p5t13, 2026-11-08).
- A walk map over the giant builds in under 1 s in Edit: survey causes with it before pricing a walk-layer fix. Single-ray slope walks from x 0 snag on his centre strip; use `bodySizeSlopeAt` (p5t15, 2026-11-08).
- A card's 'no X on Y' check needs a ray grid over Y's top vs the layer (bounds-box overlap alone can't say above or below); p5t15 found steep pieces 0.65/1.28 above the belly pocket ledges that way.
- The cuff strap/pocket art (Mockup_Nests.Art) is CanQuery off: an Include-filter ray onto it never hits. WalkAreaFacts' float and seed rays had this bug (30 false floats); measure art by CFrame/Size (p5t15c, 2026-11-09).
- A check's sample rule names every edge of its area, not only the seam: Task 14's "pyjama above the seam" had no north edge and gave 552 fake chest gaps. Walk area on his body ends at z −20 for |x| < 22 (spec line 83, p5t15c Q1).
- A reach flood follows every link kind Play allows (Walk+Slide+Climb), and its no-change control must match a Play result before the changed run counts. `roomToStand` at a raw skin sample needs the skin normal as `up`, or every curved spot reads "blocked by itself" (p5t15d Step 2, 2026-11-09).
- Before choosing a walk's start, ray along its line: 15d W4 started touching the invisible RailGuard and read NO MOVE (rail at z -50.8; start z -51.4). Start 4+ studs before any guard (p5t15d, 2026-11-09).
- To list a Jest-only check in Edit, clone its modules with the JestGlobals line swapped for a stub, and clone every changed module too: a cached old WalkAreaCheck gave 913 problems instead of 309 (p5t15g Step 5, 2026-11-09). A trace must use the test's own map: 15g's trace used Belly boxes (area 596) and missed 289 gaps the scan map shows (area 324 of 2130 belly spots).
- Walk-off starts on the belly side: 3 studs inside the seam all read "too steep to hold still"; ray down from y 90 at 6 in, then `nearSpot(hit + 0.5, 2)`, gave open starts. A ray from y 90 near the ramp top hits the belly above it, not the ramp (p5t15e, 2026-11-09).
- Art copied out of `Mockup_` keeps `MockupCanQuery` false, so world rays miss it: a "walk top over visible art" test uses ray-box math on each piece's box (p5t15e3, 2026-11-09).
- `linkProblem`'s body box tilts with a 45° ramp. Where the ramp meets steeper skin, its corner brushes the skin a real upright player clears. Before ruling, rerun the link with that part in `boxIgnores` and ray up from each sample, then look for an older Play walk there (p3t8c Rare egg 1, 2026-11-10).
- Probe a link with the end points the real script builds (its own ground ray from the spot), never an old route point: a 0.1 stud height change hid a second blocker at Rare egg 1, and ruling 1 was wrong (p3t8c, 2026-11-10).
- Never mirror meadow walk points: props are not mirrored. Run the prop clearance on both sides before a card relies on mirroring (p3t8c, 2026-11-10: all 6 prop failures on the right). To list every failure at once, require a clone of the check with its error() calls patched to collect.
- A point search that works in one call: grid candidates (terrain only), a prop-only shortest chain first, then `linkProblem` on just that chain's links, banning a failing pair and rerunning; trail lengths by shortest path over `Places.LINKS` (p3t8c2, 2026-11-10: 1 call, all pass).
- Never compare a route length ÷ 16 with a walked time: walks cut corners by 2-5%. Compare route with route (Rule 35 pairs). A route point is a grounded spot: read its zone from zone boxes only, never climberZone (plan line 46).
- A search that requires a tools/ module (not a clone of the whole folder) can get Studio's old cached copy: p3t8 fine search read the pre-8c2 points. Clone the whole folder (`folder:Clone().X`) or gsub every `require(script.Parent.Y)` to a clone, and print one known point first. Same for src/ client modules (p3t8f: live Hud.Layout gave the old tilt 12; `Hud:Clone()` gave -12). Print the value the task changed.
- BillboardGui.MaxDistance culls by camera depth (viewport Z), not real distance: at 104 real studs, depth 72 the word drew with MaxDistance 75 (p5fix2 stack2, 2026-11-10). A label rule gated on real distance must hide far words itself; read AbsoluteSize, real distance and viewport Z in one client read first.
- An offline path past 3 named props is not a promise: p3t8 fine search found 0.35 of the 2.3 studs it predicted. Price a "nudge the points" fix only after a Studio search with every prop.
- Prompt-distance rules (Rule 36, Spot rule 4) measure from a standable top (ray hit, normal Y>0.75, +3 for the root), never from a route centerline or hidden Fill pieces: p5t17 knee pockets read 9.1/3-8 red but really 6.0 plank / 9.9 ramp (2026-11-10).
