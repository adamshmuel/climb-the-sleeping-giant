# Play jobs in this game

- Noise measure: wait until the meter is under 5 first. Keep watching 3 s after the player stands still and read the highest value (a thud counts only after 0.3 s at rest). A slip is a jump, and every jump thuds 20 times the zone's multiplier.
- A body over 20 studs from the test spot is SENT AWAY: log it, retry once, never count a fail. Split a rise into its parts (end of climb, landing) so a loud part can be named.
- A climb judged at the run's last row: size the push from a ray of the top's width, so the body stops on top (6a3c: W 8000 walked off the cap; 3 s fits).
- A walk test's "nobody in" covers only the edges its tries pressed: before ruling, ray the gap under every edge of the piece (6b: knee-side lap half of both planks 5-11.6 high, untried, 2026-11-08). A floor log naming look parts (Rung, Hook) is the floor ray ignoring CanCollide, not a route fault.
