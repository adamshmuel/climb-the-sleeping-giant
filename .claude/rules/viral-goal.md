# The goal: a game that can go viral

This is the main reason for making this game. Every other rule serves it.

## The viral test

Every decision about how the game looks, feels or plays answers two questions, with evidence (real gameplay screenshots of the top games next to ours, or a play of the build):

1. **Could it go viral looking and feeling like this?** Would players share it, come back to it, and bring their friends?
2. **Would a kid pick ours over the top game next to it?**

## Where it applies

- **Everything the player sees, feels or plays:** ideas, designs, mockups, specs, gameplay rules, redesigns, cuts and built features.
- **Every review and every approval** of those, by every agent.
- **Every cheaper or simpler choice.** Saving time is good, but a choice that saves time and fails the test is not allowed. Time saved goes to what makes the game go viral.
- **The main chat** asks both questions again before passing any approval to Adam, and before any gate.

## What the answer means

| Answer | What happens |
|---|---|
| Yes, with evidence | Go ahead |
| No, or not sure | Changes, even if every other rule passes |
| Yes, but only if something changes | That change becomes part of the decision |

A report that makes a look or gameplay decision says its viral-test answer in one line, and why.
