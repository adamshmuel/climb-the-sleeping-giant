# Look study: Climb the Sleeping Giant

What the top Roblox games really look like in play, and what that means for our game.

- **Studied on:** 2026-10-11.
- **Source:** real gameplay screenshots in the shared reference library, `references/top-games/` (28 games, filled the same day). Every point below names the screenshots it comes from.
- **Live chart numbers:** fetched 2026-10-11 at 19:08 UTC with the `roblox-trends` skill. "Upvotes" means the share of players who gave the game a thumbs-up.

## The games we compared

| Group | Games | Why |
|---|---|---|
| The bar Adam set | Adopt Me | Adam compared our last build with it. |
| Our closest competitors | Steal An Egg (1,314,181 playing, 92% upvotes), Break and Steal an Egg (92,105, 97%), Don't Steal the Bobo, Dont Wake the Kitty, Dont Wake Up for Eggs | Same "grab an egg from a guard" loop. |
| Best-looking egg game | Ride A Pet (73,288 playing, 96%) | Proves a rich world works in this genre. |
| One huge creature vs small players | 99 Nights in the Forest, Fisch | Our twist is scale. |
| A world made of giant everyday objects | Speed Keyboard Escape | Our teacup, sock and candle. |
| Busy screens at the top of the charts | Brookhaven, Blox Fruits | How much a screen can hold. |

## 1. World: how full it is

| What we saw | Screenshots |
|---|---|
| **Adopt Me is full but simple.** Low-poly trees, bright shop fronts, signs, gift boxes, roads. Big areas are plain grey road. The fullness comes from buildings with signs and from people. | `adopt-me/shot-01.jpg`, `shot-02.jpg`, `shot-05.jpg`, `shot-07.jpg`, `shot-08.jpg` |
| **Ride A Pet looks the richest of the egg games.** Real terrain, chunky cartoon trees, wooden fences, big detailed creatures, warm light. | `ride-a-pet/shot-01.jpg`, `shot-03.jpg`, `shot-04.jpg` |
| **The steal-egg games are flat.** Green studded floors, plain walls, coloured pads, blocky animals. The world is a board, not a place. | `steal-an-egg/shot-01.jpg`, `shot-08.jpg` to `shot-10.jpg`, `break-and-steal-an-egg/shot-06.jpg`, `dont-steal-the-bobo/shot-02.jpg` to `shot-05.jpg`, `dont-wake-the-kitty/shot-02.jpg` |
| **Giant everyday objects make a world feel special at once.** A floor of keyboard keys, giant sweets and pumpkins. | `speed-keyboard-escape/shot-01.jpg`, `shot-02.jpg` |
| **What moves:** other players, pets following them, floating name tags, sparkles over rare things, coins and numbers popping up. | `adopt-me/shot-01.jpg`, `shot-03.jpg`, `steal-an-egg/shot-09.jpg`, `ride-a-pet/shot-02.jpg` |

**For us:** our edge is a real place (terrain, stream, trees, flowers, giant objects), at Ride A Pet's level or above, not a studded board. Fullness must also come from people and pets, not only props.

## 2. Characters and creatures

| What we saw | Screenshots |
|---|---|
| Players are plain blocky Roblox avatars in every game. | all |
| Pets and creatures are **chunky, round and cute**, with big eyes. Rare ones glow or sparkle. | `adopt-me/shot-06.jpg`, `dont-steal-the-bobo/shot-03.jpg`, `ride-a-pet/shot-04.jpg` |
| The guards in the steal games are **voxel animals** (built from cubes), about 2 to 4 times player size. None is truly huge. | `steal-an-egg/shot-08.jpg`, `shot-10.jpg`, `break-and-steal-an-egg/shot-06.jpg` |
| **Scale sells danger.** A giant shark rising over a tiny fisher on a dock. A deer monster with a searchlight beam stalking players across a dark field. | `fisch/shot-02.jpg`, `99-nights-in-the-forest/shot-03.jpg` |

**For us:** the giant must fill the screen from the player's spot, and stay cute: round shapes, rosy cheeks, soft colours. Pets are round toy-like creatures, as cute as Adopt Me's.

## 3. Colours and light

| What we saw | Screenshots |
|---|---|
| Daytime, almost always. Bright blue sky, saturated green grass, warm sunlight. | `adopt-me/shot-01.jpg`, `ride-a-pet/shot-03.jpg`, `steal-an-egg/shot-08.jpg` |
| Ride A Pet adds soft shadows and a warm haze in the distance, which gives depth. | `ride-a-pet/shot-03.jpg`, `shot-04.jpg` |
| The steal games use pure flat colours (lime green, hot red, purple) with no shading. It reads on a phone but looks cheap. | `steal-an-egg/shot-09.jpg`, `dont-steal-the-bobo/shot-05.jpg` |
| Night scenes only in survival and horror games. | `99-nights-in-the-forest/shot-02.jpg`, `shot-03.jpg` |

**For us:** sunny afternoon, warm light, soft shadows, light blue haze far away. Bright, but shaded, never flat.

## 4. Screen layout

| What we saw | Screenshots |
|---|---|
| **Left side: a stack or grid of square menu buttons**, each with a picture and a word (Shop, Index, Rebirth, Gear, Trade). | `dont-steal-the-bobo/shot-02.jpg` to `shot-05.jpg`, `ride-a-pet/shot-01.jpg`, `steal-an-egg/shot-08.jpg` |
| **Adopt Me puts its menu on the right edge**, small round icons with tiny words. | `adopt-me/shot-01.jpg`, `shot-05.jpg` |
| **Cash is big and bold**, with a coin or note picture: top right (Adopt Me) or bottom left (egg games). | `adopt-me/shot-01.jpg`, `steal-an-egg/shot-08.jpg`, `ride-a-pet/shot-03.jpg` |
| **Countdowns sit near the top** ("Guaranteed Legendary in 03:04", "Guaranteed Mythic in 01:55"). | `steal-an-egg/shot-05.jpg`, `dont-steal-the-bobo/shot-03.jpg` |
| **Big action words appear in the middle-low area** (Drop, Pick Up, Hatch Now!). | `dont-steal-the-bobo/shot-03.jpg`, `adopt-me/shot-10.jpg` |
| **The bottom corners stay free** for the thumbstick and Jump on phones. | `adopt-me/shot-08.jpg`, `shot-09.jpg`, `blox-fruits/shot-01.jpg` |
| **Buttons have a thick dark outline, a bright fill and a white or yellow word with an outline.** That is what makes them look finished. | `dont-steal-the-bobo/shot-02.jpg`, `ride-a-pet/shot-01.jpg`, `dont-wake-the-kitty/shot-02.jpg` |
| **What goes wrong:** chat, name tags and ads pile up in the middle and cover the action. | `adopt-me/shot-01.jpg`, `shot-03.jpg`, `dont-steal-the-bobo/shot-05.jpg` |

**For us:** menu grid on the left, cash top right, the giant's sleep meter and the event countdown top centre, actions on the right, nothing in the middle of the screen except short messages.

## 6. What the top games share, and what lower-rated games do wrong

| Shared by the winners | Seen in |
|---|---|
| One clear hero thing on screen (a pet, an egg, a creature). | all |
| Bright, warm daytime colours. | all except horror |
| Chunky labelled buttons with outlines. | egg games, Adopt Me |
| Something always moving: pets, players, sparkles, numbers. | all |

| Lower-rated games (live upvotes, 2026-10-11) | What hurts the look |
|---|---|
| Steal a Brainrot, 85% | Same flat studded template as the rest. |
| Shoot For Eggs, 85% | Template look. |
| Steal A Verity, 71% | Template look. |
| Dont Wake Up for Eggs, 79% (from the idea's research) | Studded floor, voxel creatures, and paid offers ("Starter Pack", "x2 Speed") stacked down the right edge all the time. `dont-wake-up-for-eggs/shot-01.jpg` to `shot-04.jpg` |

The highest-rated ones (Break and Steal an Egg 97%, Ride A Pet 96%, Clone to Steal Eggs 97%) are rated on fun, not looks, but Ride A Pet shows that a rich world and a high rating go together.

## 7. Store icons and thumbnails

| What we saw | Files |
|---|---|
| Icons are **polished renders**, much nicer than the games themselves: one big character, one big egg, a bright background, often a short word ("YOU", "STEAL"). | `store-icon.png` in `steal-an-egg`, `break-and-steal-an-egg`, `dont-steal-the-bobo`, `ride-a-pet`, `adopt-me` |
| The sneak games show the **sleeping guard** with "zZZ". | `break-and-steal-an-egg/store-thumb.png`, `dont-steal-the-bobo/store-thumb.png` |
| Thumbnails use 2 to 4 big words with thick outlines, and bright red arrows or "!!". | `steal-an-egg/store-thumb.png`, `dont-steal-the-bobo/store-thumb.png` |

**For us:** Roblox's rules say thumbnails must show the real game (see the style guide). So our icon and thumbnails are real Play screenshots with big words added. That keeps them honest, but they will look less glossy than a render. See `build-check.md`.
