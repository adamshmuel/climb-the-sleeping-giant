## Colors

- **Sky:** bright cyan-blue, almost always. It is the default background of the whole category.
- **Ground:** saturated lime grass, often with the Roblox stud pattern showing.
- **Accents:** one hot color per image: lava orange, rainbow, magenta galaxy, gold. Rare things get rainbow or purple-pink glow.
- Night or dark scenes are rare. The only ones are Steal An Egg's sleeping-dragon thumbnail (purple space) and Steal A Verity's forest at night.

## Lighting

- Bright midday light, soft shadows, a white rim light around characters so they pop off the background.
- Rare eggs and pets glow (lava cracks, sparkles, light bursts). Glow is the main "this is valuable" signal.

## Characters

- Almost every image uses the same default-style avatar: brown "bacon" hair, black jacket, blue shirt. Players recognize it as "me, a Roblox player".
- Avatars are blocky R15 bodies with big expressive faces (smirk, crying, scared). The face carries the emotion.
- Creatures are either blocky (built from parts, like Break and Steal an Egg's chicken or Grow a Garden's crops) or smooth and stylized (Steal An Egg's lion, Steal Animal Egg's chubby white bird).
- **Steal Animal Egg's chubby round bird** is the closest thing to our giant: round, soft, silly, and it reads instantly at small size.

## Camera angles

- Low angle, close to the avatar. The avatar fills about a third of the frame, the creature or egg fills another third.
- The avatar is in the front corner, mid-run, holding the egg. The creature is behind, big and threatening.
- Almost no one shows the real third-person gameplay camera. Ride A Pet's flying shot is the exception.

## Effects

- "zZZ" letters over a sleeping creature (Steal An Egg, Break and Steal an Egg). Players already understand "sleeping monster = sneak".
- Red "!!" and "?!" marks for danger or surprise.
- A red curved arrow pointing at the avatar with the word "YOU!".
- Speed lines, dust clouds behind running feet, sparkles around rare items.

## Text on icons and thumbnails

- **Icons:** mostly image-only (Steal An Egg, Steal Animal Egg, Break and Steal an Egg, Ant Empire, Drill for Eggs). A few put a huge number or a "NEW" tag on the icon.
- **Thumbnails:** big, chunky, outlined text. The common types:
  - rarity names in rainbow gradient ("SECRET", "MYTHIC", "Transcendent");
  - money per second in green ("$89,121/s", "$997,672/s");
  - one short hook ("YOU!", "GROWS OFFLINE", "999 MPH").
- Text sits at the top or the top corners, never at the bottom. Roblox's own guide says the bottom can be covered by the player count.

## What the top games share

1. One clear action in one picture: avatar + creature + egg.
2. A big, emotional avatar face.
3. Bright blue sky and green ground, so the image reads at tiny size.
4. Glow on the valuable thing.
5. A hint of danger (a sleeping or angry creature) with the player getting away.

## What the low-rated games do wrong

| Game | Upvotes | What the images do |
|---|---|---|
| Steal A Verity! | 72% | Creepy grinning horror face and a Minecraft-style pixel world. The look doesn't match "Roblox", and the scary face fights the cute steal formula. |
| Build a Phone Empire | 76% | No avatar and no game world at all, only phone product art. You can't tell what you do in the game. |
| Paint to Get Rich | 71% | Busy images with a lot of text ("$1 vs $999M"), and a famous painting. Message is "get rich", not "this is fun to play". |

Upvotes come from gameplay, not only images, so this is a pattern, not proof.

## A warning about the category

Most top thumbnails are polished 3D renders that look far better than the actual game. Roblox's thumbnail rules say graphics must represent what players will really see. For Sneak the Nest, every image must look like something Adam can build in Studio (see `style-guide.md`, Roblox build limits).

## What this means for Sneak the Nest

- **Stand out with the giant, not the egg.** Every competitor's thumbnail is "avatar holding egg". Ours is "a team of avatars tiptoeing past one huge sleepy giant".
- **Show more than one player.** No competitor thumbnail shows a team. That alone makes ours look different and sells the co-op twist.
- **Keep the category's readability** (bright colors, glow on eggs, chunky outlined text, big faces) but use a **soft sunset** instead of flat noon blue. It still reads on a phone, and it says "sleepy".
- **Use the signals players already know:** "zZZ" for sleeping, "!" for danger.
- **Skip** horror faces, Minecraft-style pixel worlds, money numbers on the icon, and walls of text.
