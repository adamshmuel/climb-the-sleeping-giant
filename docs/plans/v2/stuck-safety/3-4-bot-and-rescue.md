# Stuck safety plan, Parts 3 and 4: the bot walk, and getting a player out (Tasks 8 to 16)

Part of the stuck safety build plan (V2, spec 2). Read the map first: `docs/plans/v2/stuck-safety.md` ("Words used here", and which file holds which task).

**How every Studio and Play step in Tasks 8 to 16 runs** (code_lead, 2026-10-27, checked against the lessons of that day, Lesson 30):
- **Start checks (Lesson 21):** every walk, leg, push, rescue try and shot starts from an open spot: a body-sized probe part (`Config.Stuck.BodyWidth` x `BodyHeight`), its bottom 0.1 above the ground a down-ray hits, tested with `Workspace:GetPartsInPart` (never `GetPartBoundsInBox`) and destroyed in the same call. A camera is placed by a ray from the player's head out to the camera.
- **Background work (Lessons 22 and 29):** long Play work (the bot walk, rescue tries, the notch proof) runs in `task.spawn`, keeps its results and a `done` flag with its seconds in `shared`, and is fetched once, after a wait in Bash for its usual time.
- **Short output (Lessons 23 and 25):** every script returns at most about 20 lines and lists at most 5 items plus a count; full tables go to `work/`. A script is pasted once, straight into Studio. The memory hook stops a builder at its limit.
- **One record of places (Lesson 28):** spots a Play proof visits (rare spots, cap spots) are read from `RARE_SPOTS` (`src/tests/server/World/StuckScan/OnTheGiant.spec.luau`; it moves into its own module if a tool needs it), never copied.
- **Every close (Lesson 27):** the full run's stuck scan is compared with the planned reds in `docs/progress/index.md`.
- **A failed spot (Lessons 19 and 26):** its cause is confirmed on about 5 real examples, read-only, before a fix is chosen; no part is fitted to a curve.

### Part 4: Getting a player out

**Task 9: Safe spots** (done, approved 2026-10-28; full run 2739 passed, 0 failed)
- Spec rules: Rule 27 (Safe spots are really safe); its numbers in spec section 5 ("Safe spot saved every": 0.5 s; "Rescue distance": 4 studs); the walk map's 70° limit (spec "Words used here", the same `StarterPlayer.CharacterMaxSlopeAngle` that `PathCheck` uses); the edge cases "A player joins and their first spot on the giant traps them" and "A player is stuck on a ladder" (a ladder spot is never saved).
- What it's for: core gameplay. Every rescue and every Reset on the giant (Tasks 10 to 13) lands on the spot this module picks. Size: 3 builder steps, 7 Studio calls (handoff `work/handoff-t9.md`).
- Files: `src/server/Players/SafeSpots.luau` (new, pure), `src/tests/server/Players/SafeSpots.spec.luau` (new), `src/shared/Config.luau` (`Stuck.SafeSaveSeconds = 0.5`, `Stuck.RescueDistance = 4`, with their type lines), `src/tests/shared/Config.spec.luau` (two tests).
- What it does: at most once every 0.5 seconds per player, it checks one sample (the time, the feet, the walking direction, the ground's surface direction, the body's state, whether `Floater` has them, and a room check passed in as a function). It saves the spot only while the body is walking or standing (`Running` or `RunningNoPhysics`), not floating or bounced, on ground no steeper than the slope limit it was made with (70°), and with room for the body. A spot is saved only if it is at least 1 stud from the last one, and the last 40 are kept (both are code_lead's numbers, which players can't see: Lesson 5). The room check runs last, only when everything else would save, so standing still costs nothing. `pick(from)` gives the newest saved spot at least 4 studs (`Config.Stuck.RescueDistance`) from `from`, or nil. `forget` drops a player's spots.
- Not here (Task 10 wires it): who feeds the samples (the ground ray, `PathCheck.roomToStand`, `Floater:isFloating`), and calling `forget` when a player leaves or gets a new character, so a new climb never starts from the last climb's spots (the same as a join, spec section 8).
- Failing tests first: a spot is saved on flat ground with room; none while falling, jumping, climbing, floating, with no room, with no ground, or on 71° ground (69° saves; Lesson 41: never test exactly 70); a second check 0.3 s later saves nothing, 0.5 s later does; a spot 0.9 studs from the last isn't saved, 1.1 is; the room check isn't called when nothing would be saved; the oldest spot goes when there are 40; the facing is saved flat, and a standing sample keeps the last walking direction; `pick` skips spots closer than 4 studs; `pick` with nothing far enough, or for an unknown player, gives nil; `forget` empties a player; two players' spots stay apart.
- Whole-session test: Task 16's `StuckSession.spec.luau` plays a brand-new player through the real `SafeSpots` (tdd.md). A pure module has no session of its own.
- Done when: the tests pass, `./scripts/check.sh` is green, and the full background run has no new fails.

**Task 10: Moving a player back, silently** (☑ done, approved 2026-10-29)
- Spec rules: Rule 31 (Being stuck is silent), Rule 27 (Safe spots are really safe), Rule 33 (What a rescue looks like), the server's part.
- Files: `src/server/Players/Recovery.luau` (new: the saving and the move), `src/server/Players/PlayerTracker.luau`, `src/shared/Net.luau` (a new `Rescued` remote), `src/client/Player/Recovery.luau` (new: the device's own move; Task 11 adds the Reset button), both `init` files, tests. `LandingDetector` doesn't change: the move resets its state with `LandingDetector.new` (code_lead, handoff `work/handoff-t10.md`). Size: 5 builder steps, 23 Studio calls, with a Play check on computer and phone.
- What changes:
  - **Saving the spots (Task 9's caller):** every 0.5 s, for each player with a character, `Recovery` gives `SafeSpots` one sample: the feet, the walking direction, the ground's surface direction from one down-ray (nil with no ground), the humanoid's state, `Floater:isFloating`, and `PathCheck.roomToStand` as the room function. It calls `forget` when a player leaves and when they get a new character.
  - **The move:** the server places the whole character on the safe spot, 0.5 studs up, facing the way they last walked, with no speed left (like `Catcher` does), and fires `Rescued` (player, where they were, where they go) to everyone. The rescued player's own device also places its character there, because a device can undo a server move of its own character (`docs/plans/core-heist.md`, "Things learned", Task 44 review row).
  - **No noise from the move:** the tracker is told the player was moved, so the move counts as neither walking nor a fall, and the small drop never thuds.
  - **No noise from shaking in a gap:** a player counts as moving only if their body went at least 1 stud in a straight line over the last second. A body shaking in a gap goes nowhere, so it makes no walking noise.
- Failing tests first: "a death on the giant keeps the safe spot picked before the spots are cleared" (product's condition, `work/t28/product-ruling.md` lines 358 to 364): when a character dies, `Recovery` first calls `SafeSpots:pick` from where it died and keeps that spot for the player, and only then, when the new character arrives, calls `forget`; the test checks the kept spot is the old one and `SafeSpots:count` is 0 afterwards. Task 13 uses the kept spot. Also: a moved player adds no walking noise on the next tick and no thud when they land; a player shaking 0.6 studs back and forth every tick makes no noise; a normal walker, a tiptoer and a ladder climber still do.
- Done when: the tests pass, and every existing noise test still passes (any changed number is listed in the report).
