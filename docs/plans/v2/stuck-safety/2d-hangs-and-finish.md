**Task 23b: The pillow's back gets a way out** (split from Task 23 on 2026-10-21, after the last guard fit failed; built after Task 28 since 2026-10-26, before Task 7)
- Spec rules: Rule 20 (One collision source), checks 1 (No stuck spots) and 4 (Feet look right). Rule 21 (The stuck scan runs on every test run). Rule 22 (What counts as stuck), including "every Climbing hang a player reaches without a jump is fixed".
- **Why the approach changed (code_lead, measured in Edit on 2026-10-21, read-only, trial parts removed):**
  1. **The pillow's back is an almost flat face at 69°.** That is just under the 70° a player can stand on (StarterPlayer's `CharacterMaxSlopeAngle` is 70). It reads 69° at every stud from z 65 to 71, at x −16.8, 1.2 and 13.2. So its 34 spots are real places to stand, and a player can walk on the back.
  2. **The trap is at the bottom, not on the back.** Below the back, the pillow rolls over into a bulge. The bulge is widest at about y 58, z 75.6, and tucks back in to touch the grass at about z 72. The grass there slopes down toward the pillow (28° to 37°). So a slide off the back drops into the corner under the bulge. The walk map finds no spot there: under the bulge a body has no room, and just outside it the scan's body, tilted with the grass, leans into the bulge. That is why all 34 spots have no way out. Pillow spot 1's hang (step 23-5: 5 of 10 drops hung, about 4.7 studs over the grass) is the same bulge, at its +x end.
  3. **Guards on the back can't work, and wouldn't help.**
     - A board steep enough (75° or more) drifts about 2 studs off a 69° face over the back's 22-stud height. That breaks product's 1-stud float limit (test 5).
     - Cutting it into tiles leaves a lip of 0.33 to 0.58 studs facing up at every row (step 23-8a.1c). That is the kind of edge Roblox's climbing grabs.
     - Even with working guards, players would still slide down into the same corner under the bulge.
  4. **Rejected too:** flush patches (a flush patch on a 69° face is still a place to stand); an invisible fill under the bulge (in the trial it took the route away from one back spot: Task 22's lesson, a fill can open a new trap); one smooth board at 72° to 74° (that is where players hung).
  5. **The trial that picked the fix:** a temporary flat part standing in for level ground at y 51 along the foot (z 72 to 79). With it, three back spots (the bottom row and higher rows) got a walk-map route out to the meadow. Without it, none did.
- What changes:
  1. **No guards on the back.** The 38 back tiles from step 23-8a are dropped. Players can stand on the back and walk down it, as they can today.
  2. **A grass shelf at the pillow's foot.** The meadow ground (Terrain, the same grass as around it) along the foot of the back is raised into a level shelf. It fills the corner under the bulge and reaches out to where the grass already stands at the shelf's height (about z 79), so the outer edge has no step. It runs the width of the back, with a gentle blend at each end. Its height follows the grass at its outer edge (about y 49 to 51 along the back). The world script builds it in a new section 4c of `tools/world/15-real-shape.luau`, with `Terrain:FillBlock` and `Terrain:FillWedge`. The section's "already done" check reads the ground height at named points. Before its first run, the section copies the ground it will change (`Terrain:CopyRegion`) into `ServerStorage.T23bTerrainBackup`, so a failed run can be put back with `Terrain:PasteRegion`. The copy is removed once the full run (step 23b-10) passes.
  3. **Pillow spot 1's corner guard** moves here from Task 23 (7 parts, fitted in step 23-8a and passed). It goes in `World.WalkSurfaces.SlideGuards`, named `SlideGuard_PillowCorner_<n>`. The shelf stays clear of it.
  4. **Parts:** 7. The shelf is ground, not parts.
- Failing tests first:
  1. (StuckScan) "the pillow's back walks out by the walk map": the lowest spot of each of the 12 pillow groups, named with its position, has a walk-map route to a meadow exit. Red today.
  2. (RealWorld) "the ground at the pillow's foot is a level shelf": at named points along the foot, the first solid ground under each point is within 0.5 studs of the shelf height there, and no steeper than 20°. Red today. The 20° is a starting value: if the scan or Play needs a flatter shelf, the measured result sets it.
  3. Already red for the pillow, and green at the end: the stuck scan (its 34 pillow spots), test 2's pillow boxes, and test 6's line for pillow spot 1 (it must hit a guard).
- Done when: the stuck scan finds zero stuck spots apart from the listed rare spots, with every V1 stuck spot checked by name; every test passes; no free spot with a way out is lost and no new stuck spot appears, anywhere; the script's second run says "already done"; Play on the computer and the iPhone 14 (sound muted): from every pillow group, a player walks or slides down the back, drops onto the shelf and walks out to the meadow within the walk-out limit, with zero `Climbing` hangs and no wedge; pillow spot 1's 10 drops have no hang; pushing into the bulge from the shelf in 4 directions gives no hang; feet on the shelf's grass look right; the phone cost again, the same way as Task 28; Task 28's jump check, re-run with the corner guard, passes; designer's yes on the shelf shots; code_lead approves only after designer's yes.

With this fix, a player can stand on the back and walk down it, then slides off the steep bottom onto the shelf.
