### Part 2: One collision source

**Task 5: His real collision shape, and the smooth-wrap trial**
- Spec rules: Rule 20 (One collision source): "The build first shows his real collision shape in Studio (with screenshots)", then "Smooth wrap".
- Files: `tools/world/13-hull-trial.luau` (new). No game code.
- What it does:
  1. Screenshots of his real collision shape, with Studio's collision view on (`PhysicsSettings.ShowDecompositionGeometry`), from the front, the side, the belly path and the forehead. code_lead's Studio test on 2026-10-19 found that a body-sized box at the middle of his belly, (0, 40, 10), touches nothing: his collision is hollow inside.
  2. Sets his solid body pieces to the smooth wrap (`CollisionFidelity` Hull, set in Edit mode: a running game can't change it), runs the stuck scan and the real-world tests, records the results, then puts everything back as it was.
- Failing test first: none (a trial). The results table is the proof.
- Done when: the report has the screenshots, the stuck spot count with the smooth wrap, which world tests failed with it, and a check that the place is back as it was.
- **Built and approved (2026-10-19, first review).** code_lead ran the saved script itself, both ways, and got the same numbers. What the screenshots show: with his real setting (`Default`) each body piece is a patchwork of many flat-sided pieces, rough under the belly path, and hollow inside (a camera inside his belly sees only the inner walls). With the smooth wrap (`Hull`) each piece is one smooth blob that bridges every dip: between his legs, under his arm, between chin and chest, and between his cap and head. The results:

|---|---|---|

| Stuck spots on the pillow (`World.GiantsThings.Pillow`, not part of him) | 36 | 35. code_lead also tried the smooth wrap on the pillow alone: 30 left, so it doesn't fix them |

**Task 6: The teacup's bowl**
- Spec rules: Rule 22 (What counts as stuck): the 4 stuck spots in the teacup's bowl (ruled real at the Task 18 review). Rule 20 (One collision source) is Tasks 19 to 24.
- Files: `tools/world/14-teacup-tea.luau` (new), `src/tests/server/World/RealWorld.spec.luau` (new tests).
- **Why the tea changed (2026-10-19).** The builder measured the rim before changing anything: it isn't level. It tilts 8.8°, highest to the west and north-west (lowest crest 18.35, highest 23.0). The smooth wrap's top (the "lid") tilts with it. A flat tea 0.3 below the lowest rim point would sit about 3 studs under a player's feet. designer chose a tea tilted like the rim, placed by the rim, not by the lid: the tea must never show above the rim, and that matters more than how close the feet are (`docs/specs/v2/research/decisions-plans.md`, "Stuck safety Task 6: the tea on a tilted rim"). That replaces the first tea rules and the first feet range (0.2 to 0.5).
- What the script does (code_lead's ruling at the Task 18 review, and designer's tea):
  1. **The smooth wrap for the cup:** sets the teacup's collision (`World.GiantsThings.Teacup.body.body_geom`) to `CollisionFidelity` `Hull` (Roblox's smooth wrap of the mesh). It fills the open bowl up to the rim, so no body fits inside. Accepted side effects: it also fills the gap between the cup and its handle (a small invisible wall), and it fills the saucer's dish, because the cup and the saucer are one mesh. The saucer becomes a steep invisible slope ("skirt") from its outer edge up to the cup, so a player walks round the outside of the saucer, and one who jumps onto it slides back off. designer accepted the skirt at the Task 6 review: no nest, egg or footprint route is near it (garden 6's nests are 21.8 and 28.8 studs from the cup's middle; route points Meadow_10 and Meadow_11 are 28 to 30 away), and nothing looks different. The cup and saucer are not split into separate collision parts.
  2. **The tea:** one flat cylinder `Part` named `Tea` inside the bowl, in the teacup's model.
     - **Tilt:** parallel to the rim's plane (the least-squares plane through the rim's 72 highest points, one every 5° round the cup). Its top is k studs under that plane, where k is the farthest any of the 72 rim points sits under the plane, plus 0.1. So the tea is at least 0.1 under every rim point. Measured: k = 0.5534.
     - **Width:** a circle fitted to the inside wall at the tea's height, in the tea's own tilted plane, gives the middle; the tea's radius is that circle's radius plus 0.3, so its edge tucks into the wall and no empty bowl shows. The edge must stay at least 0.2 studs inside the cup's outer wall all the way round (measured: 0.38 at the closest).
     - **Look:** 0.4 studs thick (at right angles to the disc), colour `#9C5E34` (designer, Task 6 review: the first colour, `#C8874A`, showed as gold in the game's warm light, too close to the cash gold `#FFC93C` and the footprint yellow `#FFD43B`), `SmoothPlastic`, not see-through, anchored. `CanCollide`, `CanQuery` and `CanTouch` all off, so the smooth wrap stays the only thing players stand on.
  3. **The cup itself doesn't change:** same mesh, colours, size and place.
  - The builder measures the rim by hand in Studio before the change and writes the numbers into the script and the test as named values, with how they were measured. Why by hand: once the cup is `Hull`, the inside of the bowl can't be measured any more. While the cup is still `Default`, the script measures again live and stops with nothing changed if the numbers don't match; it also stops if the cup's size or place has changed. If the tea is already there in its old colour (`#C8874A`), the script only sets the new colour; any other colour stops it with nothing changed. A second run says "already done".
- Failing tests first (real world):
  1. The teacup's collision is `Hull`.
  2. No standing body fits in the bowl: `PathCheck.roomToStand` (the scan's own check, with the box tilted to the ground's slope) is false at the 4 stuck spots from the Task 18 review and at the bottom of the bowl, (75, 6.08, -58). Each spot is the real standing place under the scan's rounded one, with its ground slope. Today a body fits at all of them. (The exact middle, (76, -58), is 72.9° steep, so it was never a spot.)
  3. The tea: exactly one `Tea` part in the teacup, with designer's colour, material, see-through value, shape, collision values and anchoring; 0.4 thick and the measured radius plus 0.3 wide; tilted like the rim; its middle on the fitted circle's middle and its top k under the rim plane; at least 0.1 under the worst rim point.
  4. A player on top stands over the tea: rays straight down at the west (68, -58), the middle (76, -58) and the east (86, -58), seeing only what a player bumps into, land **0.4 to 1.0 studs** above the tea's top.
- Done when: the tests pass; the stuck scan lists 1332 spots, none on the teacup and none "none within 10 studs"; every other test passes; the script's second run says "already done"; and the place has unsaved world changes (Adam saves it).
- **Play check** (designer's, on a computer and the iPhone 14, sound muted): standing on the lid at the west, middle and east (put there with the Studio tools, because only a jump reaches it), the player stands over the tea, not on air, and isn't wedged: they walk off the rim to the meadow with no jump. From above, no tea shows over the rim and no empty bowl shows. Walking all the way round the outside of the saucer works, with no bump before the saucer's visible edge. From a garden, the cup looks the same as before (a before and an after shot from the same camera). **The tea's colour in the game:** the overhead shot and the shot on the lid's middle, from the same cameras as before, measured at the middle of the tea, read between about `#B87840` and `#D0955A` (clearly brown, darker than the cash gold). If darker, the colour value goes lighter a little at a time; if lighter, darker. The main chat sends the screenshots to designer.

**How his real shape is patched**
- **His real shape:** each of his six body pieces (`legs_and_boots`, `body_and_cushion`, `left_arm`, `right_arm`, `head_with_beard`, `nightcap`) gets the collision setting `PreciseConvexDecomposition`: Roblox's closest match of the mesh players see. It can only be set in Edit mode. Roblox then works out the shape in the background (about 24 seconds) and keeps it in the place file. The pieces stay solid, in the normal collision group, so the camera stops at his skin as in V1.
- **Patches:** small invisible solid blocks (or wedges) that fill or close a pocket, crack or dent the scan finds, sitting flush with his skin, so no player stands on air. Task 22.
- **Slide guards:** invisible boards tilted past 70° (steeper than a player can walk), like V1's forehead guard, so a player slides off a small steep island instead of standing trapped on it. Only outside the Belly zone, because spec 5's walk area (`egg-nests-and-climbing.md`, Rule 79: Walk anywhere down to the seam) lies inside it. Inside the Belly zone a dent gets a flush patch instead (product). Never near a place players need (game_creator). Task 23.
- **The steep layer below the seam is not a slide guard** (product, 2026-10-20). Spec 5 puts an invisible steep layer on walkable cloth just below the seam (Rule 80: The seam shows where walking ends), and that layer stays even inside the Belly zone. The rule "no slide guard in the Belly zone" covers only Rule 20's patches and slide guards (Task 23's test 3), never spec 5's steep layer (plan 5).
- **Walk pieces:** V1's invisible floors on him (the boot tops, the belly path, the nose and the nightcap) sit on his skin, 0.2 studs below to 0.7 above it over their whole top, or go where his own skin is walkable. Between his left boot top and Rare egg 1 the walk line crosses two walls, so it uses short solid **crossing ramps** there, like plan 3's belly ramps, until plan 3, Task 6 removes that stretch (Task 24, Ruling 1). Legs and belly: Task 24. Nose deck, nightcap and ladder ends: Task 25.
- **Where they live:** `World.WalkSurfaces.Patches` and `World.WalkSurfaces.SlideGuards`, next to V1's invisible walk pieces and guards. Why there: the red circle is drawn only on his pieces and the planks (`SlamFx.huggableParts`), so it lies on the skin players see, never on an invisible block, as with V1's guards. And nothing there moves: while he sleeps nothing a player stands on moves (spec 5, Rule 100), and his right arm swings only while he's awake, after everyone is bounced off (Rule 12: Waking bounces everyone off).
- **How close they sit** (designer, 2026-10-19):
  - where players stand on a patch: its top between 0.2 studs below and 0.7 above **his skin around it**;
  - on a steep face of a patch or a slide guard (steeper than 70°): at most 1 stud out from his skin around it and at most 0.5 studs in;
  - a patch never sticks out through his skin.
- **"His skin around a point"** is judged at body size, as designer ruled for his slope: the flat plane that best fits 25 rays that see only his body pieces (and the pillow, chair and cushion, which are part of them or stand next to them), on a 4 by 4 stud square around the point, 1 stud apart, sent along the patch face's own direction from 3 studs outside it. Only hits within 2 studs of the face count, so the bottom of the dent behind a patch is left out and the rim around it is kept. With fewer than 13 such hits, the square grows to 8 by 8 (81 rays). Still fewer than 13: the test fails and names the place, because a patch that wide needs designer's eyes. designer confirmed this way of measuring on 2026-10-20: counting only hits within 2 studs is safe, because designer's largest limit is 1 stud.
- **How many parts:** patches and slide guards together at most 200 (trial B used 97). Over that, the builder stops and code_lead decides. Why the limit: every phone holds them (`World.Giant` and `WalkSurfaces` stream to every player), and trial B's 97 cost too little to measure.
- **The stuck scan's body check counts the meadow ground** (Task 20). Roblox's exact overlap check (`GetPartsInPart`) doesn't see the Terrain, so the 138 "spots" inside the ground under the chair counted as room for a body. Product approved it through spec section 8.
- **One world script** holds it all, `tools/world/15-real-shape.luau`, one section per task (his real shape, then the walk pieces, then the patches, then the slide guards), each with fixed numbers, so a second run says "already done".
