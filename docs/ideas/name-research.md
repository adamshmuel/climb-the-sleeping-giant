# Name research: a new name for "Steal the Giant's Eggs"

Data fetched 2026-10-14, 06:22 UTC, from the live Roblox charts (Top Playing Now, Top Earning, Up-and-Coming, Top Trending), Roblox game details and Roblox search. Peak numbers come from the articles listed at the bottom.

Why a new name: the current name sounds hostile, and "Steal ... Eggs" looks like a copy of "Steal An Egg", the biggest game on Roblox.

Words used here:
- **Players now:** how many people were playing at the moment the data was fetched.
- **Peak:** the most people ever playing at the same time.
- **Visits:** how many times the game has been played in total.
- **Core loop:** the action players repeat again and again.

### Adam's idea, tested

"Strong names point to the main character" is **not** what the data shows. Only 1 of 30 names a character, and 0 of 30 name their monster. What the data does show:
- Names point to **what you do** (action word first) and **what you get** (the thing you collect).
- The word "Giant" is still worth keeping, for another reason: it appears in **0 of 305** chart names today. Every Roblox game with "giant" in its name has under 500 players now. It is the one word that makes us look different from the egg games, and it matches the giant in our icon.

### What Roblox itself says about names

From Roblox's official Discovery guide (how Roblox picks which games to show):
- Use original names, and avoid titles that repeat earlier games. Games that look like copies "are no longer prioritized for recommendations" (are shown less).
- The name must match the gameplay. A name that promises something the game doesn't have is shown less.
- If you follow a trend, add your own spin to the title.

This is the strongest reason to move away from "Steal ... Eggs": Roblox may treat a name like that as one more copy.

## 4. My pick

**Climb the Sleeping Giant.** It is the only option that says the action, the unique creature and the danger: "climb" (what you do), "Giant" (in no chart name today) and "Sleeping" (be quiet). It uses simple words a young or non-English kid knows, and no Roblox game uses it.

Things to know:
- The name doesn't say "eggs" or "pets", and 14 of 30 top names say what you collect or use. The icon must show it: eggs and pets on the sleeping giant.
- "Climb" is also used by obby games (climbing courses) and by the "Climb for Eggs" copy wave. The giant in the name and in the icon sets us apart.
- Starting choice, and what would change it: if the first week's click rate on the icon (the share of players who click after seeing it) is below the average for similar games in the Creator Dashboard, test "Climb the Giant" as the shorter name.
