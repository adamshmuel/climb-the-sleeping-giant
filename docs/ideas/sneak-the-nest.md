# Sneak the Nest

## Pitch

Tiptoe into a sleeping giant's nest, grab an egg, and run home before it wakes up. Eggs hatch into pets that earn cash at your base, and cash buys quieter shoes, faster legs and bigger incubators so you can reach the rare eggs near the giant's head. It's the "steal an egg" game, except you steal from a monster instead of from other players, so friends heist together and nobody gets robbed.

## Genre

Simulation > Tycoon

(Official Roblox definition: "Games simulating the management of a business or base. They often involve collecting money from 'droppers' used to upgrade the base." Here the pets are the droppers.)

## Core loop

1. **Sneak:** creep into the giant's nest without making too much noise.
2. **Grab:** pick up an egg (bigger, rarer eggs are heavier and noisier to carry).
3. **Escape:** get back over the bridge to your base, sprinting if the giant wakes.
4. **Hatch:** put the egg in an incubator, wait, and watch the reveal.
5. **Earn:** pets sit on pads at your base and make cash.
6. **Upgrade:** spend cash on quieter shoes, speed, carrying strength and more incubators and pads. That lets you reach the rarer eggs deeper in the nest.

**Fun in the first 10 seconds:** you spawn looking straight at a huge sleeping giant snoring next to a pile of glowing eggs, with an arrow showing the way. The first sneak starts right away.

## Twist

Every "steal" game makes you rob other players. Here the victim is a **sleeping giant shared by the whole server**. Anyone's noise can wake it for everybody, so a server becomes a team of nervous burglars. That gives the tension of stealing without the griefing (players bullying or robbing each other), and it makes playing with friends pay off.

## V1 gameplay

This describes what the player experiences in the first version, from their point of view.

### The first 10 seconds

- You spawn on your own small base: a patch of grass with an empty incubator and a few empty pet pads.
- Across a short wooden bridge is the nest, a giant bowl of twigs. Inside, an enormous giant is curled up asleep and snoring. Eggs glow all around it.
- A glowing trail runs from your feet to the nearest egg, and a big icon above it shows a hand grabbing an egg. No text box and no menu.
- Within a few steps you are already inside the nest.

### The first 5 minutes

- **Minute 0–1: your first heist.** The first egg sits on the outer edge of the nest. The giant is in its deepest sleep, so it's almost impossible to wake. You grab the egg, walk back over the bridge, and the trail leads you to your incubator.
- **Minute 1–2: your first hatch.** The first egg hatches fast. The egg wobbles, cracks and pops, and a pet jumps out with a little celebration. The trail leads you to a pet pad, and coins start floating up from it.
- **Minute 2–3: your first upgrade.** Coins pile up in your cash bin and you step on it to collect them. A glowing upgrade board lights up: "Quiet Shoes". You buy it and feel your footsteps get softer.
- **Minute 3–4: your first scare.** On a later heist the giant stirs for the first time. It mumbles and rolls over, and the noise meter flashes. You freeze, it settles, and you sneak out. You learn the danger without losing anything.
- **Minute 4–5: your first rare egg.** A guaranteed Rare egg appears a little deeper in the nest, closer to the giant. Grabbing it is your first real risk, and hatching it is your first "wow" moment. The Index (the collection book) opens to show how many pets are still missing.

### A full heist, step by step

1. **Entering the nest.** You cross the bridge. Once your feet touch the twigs, a noise meter appears above the giant's head. Walking slowly fills it a little, and running fills it a lot. Crunchy dry twigs (shown with a small crack mark) make extra noise. Soft moss patches are silent.
2. **Choosing an egg.** Eggs on the outer ring are common and light. Eggs in the middle ring are uncommon to rare. Eggs right next to the giant's head, on its pillow, are epic and legendary. Each egg has a colored glow so you can see its rarity before you commit.
3. **Grabbing.** You hold the grab button for a moment and the egg lifts onto your back. Heavier eggs slow you down and make every step a bit louder, so a Legendary egg is a slow, nervous walk.
4. **The giant stirs.** When the noise meter gets high, the giant gives warnings: snoring stops, one eye flutters, and a low rumble plays. If everyone freezes, the meter slowly drains and the giant falls back asleep.
5. **The giant wakes.** If the meter fills, the giant roars, sits up and starts stomping after anyone holding an egg. The screen shakes, the music changes, and you can now sprint without making things worse.
6. **Escaping.** Your goal is the bridge. Once you cross the safe line at the end of the bridge, you're home free. The giant is too big for the bridge, so it grumbles, turns around and goes back to sleep after a short while.
7. **Getting caught.** If the giant reaches you, it scoops you up, shakes the egg loose (the egg rolls back into the nest), and flicks you gently back to your base. You lose the egg you were carrying and nothing else: no cash, no pets. Then you can try again straight away.

### Hatching and pets

- **Incubators:** you start with one. Eggs hatch over a short wait: common eggs quickly, rare ones a bit longer. You can go on more heists while you wait.
- **The reveal:** every hatch has a wobble, crack and pop animation. Rarer pets get a bigger burst of light and a sound everyone nearby can hear.
- **Rarity tiers in V1:** Common, Uncommon, Rare, Epic, Legendary.
- **Pets** are small cute creatures that match the giant's world (baby birds, lizards, fluffballs). Each egg type hatches one of a few pets from its tier.
- **The Index** is a collection book showing every pet as a silhouette until you hatch it. Finishing a tier's page gives a reward.
- **Pets do two jobs:** they sit on pads and make cash, and you can have one follow you around as a companion just to show it off.

### The home base and how cash is earned

- Your base is a plot next to the bridge, with incubators, pet pads and a cash bin.
- Each pet on a pad drops coins into the cash bin. Rarer pets drop more.
- You walk over the cash bin to collect. A satisfying coin sound and counter make it feel good.
- Your base fills up visually as you grow: more pads, bigger incubators and decorations that unlock with progress. Friends who visit can see how far you've got.
- While you're offline, your pets keep earning for a limited time, so there's a welcome-back pile of coins waiting when you return.

### Upgrades and progression

| Upgrade | What it does for the player |
|---|---|
| Quiet Shoes | Each step makes less noise, so you can go deeper before the giant stirs |
| Fast Legs | You run faster when escaping |
| Strong Back | Heavy eggs slow you down less |
| More Incubators | Hatch several eggs at once |
| More Pet Pads | More pets earning at once |

How progression feels:
- **Short term (one session):** buy the next upgrade and grab a rarer egg than last time.
- **Mid term (a few days):** reach the pillow eggs next to the giant's head and fill the Rare and Epic pages of the Index.
- **Long term (weeks):** hatch every Legendary and complete the whole Index.
- **Daily reward:** log in each day for a free gift. On a streak, the gift is a free egg that skips the heist.

### Playing with friends

- **One giant per server.** Everyone sneaks into the same nest. One clumsy player can wake the giant for everybody, which is the main source of funny moments.
- **Twin Eggs.** Some of the biggest eggs are too heavy for one person. Two players carry them together, and when it hatches both players get a pet.
- **Friend bonus.** Playing in the same server as a friend gives everyone a small cash boost.
- **Lookout.** A player standing on the lookout rock at the nest's edge can see the noise meter from far away and shout a warning. It's a simple job for younger players or friends who prefer not to sneak.
- **Nobody can steal from anyone.** Your base and pets are always safe.

### Left out of V1 (saved for later)

- Stealing from other players (left out on purpose, maybe forever)
- Trading pets
- More nests and giants (new worlds)
- Rebirth (resetting progress for a permanent bonus)
- Pet sizes and mutations (special variants)
- Limited-time events and holiday giants
- Season pass
- Rewarded video ads (for example, "watch an ad to double offline cash")
- Leaderboards
- Pet abilities that help during heists
- Robux eggs: you can never buy an egg with Robux, in V1 or later (see Monetization)

## Retention

Retention means reasons for players to come back.

- **Progression:** five upgrade paths, and rarer eggs deeper in the nest that you can only reach with better gear.
- **Collection:** the Index with five rarity tiers and rewards for completing pages. The Steal An Egg badges show players chase rare hatches (35% reach the first Secret pet).
- **Friends:** a shared giant, Twin Eggs, the friend bonus and the Lookout job. Nobody gets robbed, so nobody quits angry.
- **Daily rewards:** a login streak with a free egg on streak days.
- **Welcome-back cash:** offline earnings waiting at the base.
- **Later updates:** each new nest is the same heist with a new giant, new eggs and new pets, so updates stay cheap to make. Roblox suggests updating weekly, and at least monthly.

| Extra Incubator | One permanent extra incubator | 199 | (new; tune later) |

**Developer products** (repeatable purchases):

| Product | What you get | Starting price (Robux) |
|---|---|---|
| Cash packs (small, medium, large) | Coins | 25 / 99 / 399 |
| Lullaby | Instantly puts the giant back to sleep for the **whole server** (a purchase that helps everyone) | 49 |
| Starter pack (once per player) | Coins and a cosmetic trail | 59 |

**Cosmetics:** trails and pet hats, up to about 799 for the fanciest (Infinity Trail and Event Horizon Scythe sell at 799 in Steal Animal Egg).

**Fairness rules:**
- Nothing anyone buys can hurt another player, because there's no player-vs-player stealing. Buying a boost only speeds up your own progress.
- **No Robux eggs.** Selling eggs (random pets) for Robux would make them "paid random items", which come with extra rules and upset players. Eggs only come from heists and daily rewards.
- No paid "skip this timer" buttons, because Roblox's own guide says players dislike them. Fast Hatch is a permanent pass instead.
- No pushy shop text. Use "Open Shop", not "BUY NOW".
